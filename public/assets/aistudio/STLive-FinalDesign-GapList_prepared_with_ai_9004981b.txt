  6 tabs: live / games / camera / library / inbox / workspace.

  streams:updated, room:join/state/chat/gift, inbox:*, auth:identify.

  APP-CSS/APP-JS/HOST-GUIDE). ~150 socket kinds, durable binary state + ephemeral Maps.
